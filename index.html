Built for the field

🔥

Early Wildfire Detection

BMV080 particulate and BME688 gas sensors detect smoke signatures before fires spread, enabling faster emergency response.

📡

Cellular Connectivity

Blues Notecard provides reliable LTE-M connectivity from remote deployments, no Wi-Fi required.

🧠

Decides on the Node

An on-device anomaly detector learns each spot's normal air and sends an alert the moment smoke is confirmed, not at the next upload.

🔧

Off-the-shelf Hardware

A small carrier board for an Arduino Nano ESP32, a Notecard and two Bosch sensors, powered by a 12 V battery and solar.

📊

Live Map & Incidents

Nodes on a live map with wind-driven spread estimates and NASA satellite hotspots. Incidents are emailed to hosts and tracked to an outcome.

🔓

Fully Open Source

Firmware and cloud stack under Apache-2.0, hardware under CERN-OHL-P, data and docs under CC BY 4.0.
