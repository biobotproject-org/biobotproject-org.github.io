:::note Rev A carrier board
The rev A carrier PCB takes an **Arduino Nano ESP32** and a **12 V** supply, for example a 12 V LiFePO4 battery behind a solar charge controller with a load output. The battery, solar and charger rows above describe a LiPo breadboard build and do not match rev A yet. The exact rev A board BOM (LCSC part numbers) is in [biobot-hardware](https://github.com/biobotproject-org/biobot-hardware) under `pcb/fire-detection-device/fabrication/`.
:::
